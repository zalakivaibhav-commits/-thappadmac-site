Installation

Up and running in minutes.

Set up ThappadMac and start exploring.

Step 01

Download the app

Get ThappadMac.dmg from the official download link. It's a regular Mac disk image.

Needs macOS 14 Sonoma or later. Tested on MacBook Air (M4).

Step 02

Install and approve it once

  1. Open ThappadMac.dmg and drag the app into Applications.
  2. Double-click ThappadMac. macOS says it can't verify the app. Click Done, not Move to Bin.
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.
  4. Confirm with Open Anyway and your Mac password if asked. After this it opens normally.

ThappadMac is an independent app that isn't notarized by Apple yet, which is why macOS asks once.

Step 03

Allow Input Monitoring for keyboard sounds

Only Keyboard Sounds needs a permission. Turn it on in the app and click Allow…, then switch ThappadMac on under System Settings → Privacy & Security → Input Monitoring and reopen the app.

ThappadMac only learns which key was pressed. It never stores or sends what you type.

Step 06

Troubleshooting

macOS says "ThappadMac" can't be opened

Click Done, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway next to the ThappadMac message. Confirm once.

Keyboard sounds stopped after an update

macOS ties Input Monitoring to the exact app build. Quit ThappadMac, reset the permission in Terminal, then reopen the app and click Allow… again:

tccutil reset ListenEvent com.thappadmac.app
Slaps don't make a sound

Check that the app shows Active, that Slap Detection is on and the volume isn't at zero. Try a lower sensitivity value. ThappadMac needs a MacBook with Apple's built-in motion sensor.

Hinge Piano shows "Simulation"

Your Mac's lid sensor didn't report movement, so the app uses an on-screen slider instead. Click Check for sensor again in the Hinge section to retry.

I used an older version with a setup script

Older versions installed a background service that is no longer needed. Remove it in Terminal (you'll type your own Mac password):

sudo launchctl bootout system/com.thappadmac.sensor
sudo rm /Library/LaunchDaemons/com.thappadmac.sensor.plist
I lost my license key

Open the customer portal with the email you used. Your key is there, and you can free up a Mac to move the license.
